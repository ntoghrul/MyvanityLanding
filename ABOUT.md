# MyVanity — The Skincare Assistant (Tough-Love Mode)

MyVanity is a tough-love AI skincare assistant that audits your bathroom shelf, helps you avoid common product clashes, and saves you money by forcing you to use what you already own.

This is not another gentle skincare app that blindly recommends more products. MyVanity tells you the truth.
Think “Gordon Ramsay meets a Dermatologist.”

> **Important:** MyVanity is not medical advice. It’s an informational assistant. You are responsible for your skincare decisions.

## What MyVanity Does

MyVanity starts where your skincare chaos begins: your bathroom shelf.

You take a photo of your counter — no typing, no forms. Using AI vision, MyVanity identifies your products, highlights key ingredients, and flags likely issues like redundancy, over-exfoliation patterns, or risky layering.

Then comes the roast.

MyVanity gives you a brutally honest breakdown of your shelf:
- How much money you’ve wasted on duplicates
- What’s redundant or pointless
- Which combinations are *likely* to cause irritation (especially with strong actives)

## Safety Focus (V1)

For V1, MyVanity focuses on four common “high-risk” actives:
- RETINOID
- AHA
- BHA
- BENZOYL_PEROXIDE


## Shopping Guardian

MyVanity doesn’t stop at your bathroom.

When you’re in a store and about to buy something new, scan the product. MyVanity evaluates it in the context of your current shelf and gives you a quick verdict:
- Don’t buy this — you already have something similar
- Optional — it fits, but you don’t need it yet
- Good buy — it fills a real gap
